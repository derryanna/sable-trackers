# Design notes: edge tab + panel polish

- **Edge tab instead of the floating button.** A 22×72 glass tab on the right edge with the wand icon. A transparent `::before` widens the hit area to 36 px. When the panel is open, the tab sits on the panel's left edge, shows a chevron and closes the panel. The tab is a sibling of the panel, because the panel clips overflow and is `display: none` when closed. It is placed with `right: var(--st-sable-panel-w)`, the same variable that sizes the panel. The drag/position code is gone, and `showFloatingButton` now reads «Язычок сбоку».
- **SillyTavern's 0 px `<html>`.** Every fixed box has an explicit top/right and a `dvh` height. The tab is centred with `top: 50dvh` plus `translateY(-50%)`. `top: 50%` would resolve against the 0 px `<html>` and land at 0.
- **Docked panel.** Right edge, full height. 80vw (max 420 px) on phones, 420 px on desktop, rounded left corners. The blur is applied once, on the panel. Cards no longer blur separately, which is cheaper on phones.
- **Cards.** Megumin-style glass with a thin light border and the white left accent bar. The bar also shows the mode: bright for «в промпт», faint for «показ», none for «выкл», so you can see at a glance what goes into the prompt. The mode chip is filled, outlined or dashed to match. Title icons are monochrome Font Awesome instead of emoji, closer to the reference.
- **World / threads / story.** World has an icon row (time, place, weather), then the summary, then a ВЫ line. Threads get red/amber/grey priority dots. Story shows small phase chips, then a 🌱/⏳ list with the age or due date right-aligned, using Russian plurals (1 ход / 2 хода / 5 ходов).
- **NPCs / dossiers.** Present NPCs come first, open, with a green dot. Absent ones are folded, dimmed and marked «не здесь». Fields are laid out as a key/value grid.
- **Bonds.** Each row is label | bar | number | delta. The bars are `role="meter"` divs, because the native `<meter>` looks different in every browser. Friction scales (suspicion, fear, grudge, tension) get a warm tint. A row with a change is a `<summary>`: tap it to see the reason.
- **Planner / ban list / status.** The planner is a numbered list with a «не забыть» note. The ban list is chips with the example in italics. The status is one quiet 11 px line with an ok/error dot.
- **Motion and stability.** The panel and the tab slide in over 140 ms, and nothing else animates. Reduced-motion settings are respected. A re-render keeps the scroll position and any rows the user unfolded, so nothing jumps.

# Design notes: drag between groups (T22)

- **Attached, then detached.** A member sorts live inside its folder until the pointer is 24 px outside the folder's box; a flat card sorts among the top-level blocks until it is 24 px deep inside a group (anywhere on a folded or empty one). Back home, plain sorting resumes.
- **Detached = a slot, not a ghost.** The card stays in place, lifted; one dashed 44 px slot marks the landing spot, computed by `slotFor` over the hovered block's rectangles. Packs, the People group and person cards dim; a full folder shows a badge and no slot.
- **One write per drop.** The slot records `{ folderId, anchor }` (the card it sits before); `dropIndex` turns the anchor into the index `planDrop` expects, so hidden cards, disabled packs and the People view never shift the position. The previous `{ folders, order }` go to the 5 s undo pill.
- **Cancel = re-render.** Every cancel path drops the marks and re-renders the saved order; the card gets a 150 ms settle (none at effects off). Auto-scroll is a rAF loop, 12 px per frame at most, inside 48 px bands.
